# Video script — maximum 3:00

Target runtime: **2:45**. Record the product first, then add narration and
captions. Never show API keys, browser devtools containing tokens, or admin
dashboards with personal information.

## 0:00–0:18 — The human problem

**Visual:** Fast cuts: blank document, chat thread, many Bible tabs, creator
staring at an empty canvas.

**Narration:** “Every week, millions of people try to turn a question into
something their community can experience. Their ideas live in one tool,
conversation in another, and Scripture arrives last—copied out of context.”

On-screen: **What if Scripture was present from the first idea?**

## 0:18–0:38 — Reveal Apolos

**Visual:** Open the prepared Apolos creator canvas. Pan smoothly across a few
clean nodes.

**Narration:** “Apolos is a collaborative Scripture creator studio. It is not
another Bible app. It is the workspace where teams shape studies, stories, and
guided experiences together.”

## 0:38–1:12 — YouVersion is native

**Visual:** Open version selector; show NIV, NIrV, NASB, and AMP. Select one,
open a passage, then compare another translation. Add passages to the canvas.
Pause briefly on publisher copyright and YouVersion attribution.

**Narration:** “Licensed editions come live from YouVersion Platform. The
catalog, structure, and passage text remain connected to their source, with
publisher licensing and attribution intact. Our backend validates requests and
caches only successful responses.”

On-screen: **LIVE · YouVersion Platform API**

## 1:12–1:55 — Gloo changes the workflow

**Visual:** In study chat, type a rehearsed prompt:

> /apolos Connect these passages to the fear of an uncertain future. Add one
> reflection question and a three-step path for a small group.

Show the loading state, Gloo response, and the proposed/created canvas changes.

**Narration:** “The creator does not leave the canvas to prompt a generic
chatbot. Apolos sends bounded canvas context to Gloo AI Studio. Gloo's
values-aligned inference helps turn the source material into a thoughtful path
while our backend enforces authentication, rate limits, and budgets.”

On-screen: **LIVE · Gloo AI Studio Completions V2**

## 1:55–2:20 — Collaboration

**Visual:** Second browser joins. Show cursor/presence or chat; add a note or
connection and show it update in the first browser.

**Narration:** “A teammate sees the same canvas in real time, responds, and
improves it. Scripture becomes conversation and co-creation—not broadcast.”

## 2:20–2:45 — Vision and close

**Visual:** Zoom out to the completed study, then show share/publish action.
End on logo and URL.

**Narration:** “For churches, creators, students, and communities already
building together online, Apolos makes Scripture feel like it always belonged
in the creative process. From blank canvas to shared encounter.”

On-screen:

- **Apolos**
- **Scripture, native to creation**
- `https://apolos.bible`
